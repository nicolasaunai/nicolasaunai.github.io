## Context
- 2024 : Master 2 internship, planétologie Paris

## Our work
Alexandre worked with me over spring 2024 on the project [[PHARE]]. More specifically, he designed and implemented a proof of concept fully kinetic PIC solver in [[PHARE]], by replacing Ohm's law in the current Hybrid-PIC solver by Maxwell-Ampere's equation and the Esirkepov's charge conserving current computation, and by adding electron particle populations to the code base.

## After
Following our collaboration, Alexandre started a PhD at [IPGP](https://www.ipgp.fr)
