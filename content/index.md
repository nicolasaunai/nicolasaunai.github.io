##  Research

I’m interested in astrophysical plasma physics. Although I spend most of my time solving the mysteries of the numerical world, I also enjoy the complicated real world data and spend some time trying to understand something out of the comparison between the two.

### 🚀 Ongoing Projects

- 💫 [[PHARE]] : Adaptive Mesh and Model Refinement for astroplasma physics modeling
- 🛰️ [SciQLOP](https://sciqlop.github.io) : In situ spacecraft visualization and analysis tools
- 🥭 [MANGO](https://github.com/LaboratoryOfPlasmaPhysics/mango): Magnetospheric Atlas of Normalized Geospace Observations
