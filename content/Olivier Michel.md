## Context
2019 :  internship [M2 PPF](https://www.universite-paris-saclay.fr/formation/master/physique/m2-physique-des-plasmas-et-de-la-fusion)

## Our work
Olivier worked with me during spring 2019 on modeling magnetic reconnection with 3D hybrid PIC simulations. More specifically, Olivier was investigating how an X-line can spread, its spreading speed and orientation, in asymmetric non-coplanar configurations.
This work was performed with [[PHARE]]'s ancestor [heckle](https://github.com/rochSmets/heckle).

## After
After his internship, Olivier did a PhD thesis at CEA where he was then hired as a permanent researcher
